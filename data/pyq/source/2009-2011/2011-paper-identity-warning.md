# Paper identity warning: bank records labelled 2011

The bank's 118 `MPPSC-2011-*` records were transcribed from the supplied third-party PDF labelled “MPPSC General Studies 2011”. This source is not an authenticated MPPSC paper. The PDF also contains an answer list on its final page; that list is a third-party key, not an official one.

A material identity conflict was found before importing those answers:

- [Competitions Today, dated 8 September 2010](https://competitionstoday.blogspot.com/2010/09/madhya-pradesh-psc-state-service-pre.html), describes the same ordered geography questions as the 2008 MPPSC State Service preliminary examination, held 11 January 2009. Its questions 1–38, including the unusual damaged Q7 and Q38 wording, correspond to the supplied PDF.
- [All Exam Guru search result](https://www.allexamgurublog.com/2009/04/mp-psc-state-service-pre-exam-2008.html) also dates its matching geography paper to 2008 (search index reports 12 April 2009). The page returned 404 when fetched; this secondary search-result evidence is weaker than the accessible 2010 page.
- [Self Study History](https://selfstudyhistory.com/2016/01/29/mppsc-general-studies-solved-paper-2011/) instead labels the same sequence 2011, consistent with the supplied document. Neither label is independently authenticated.

A pre-2011 publication containing the same numbered sequence is strong evidence against treating these records as an independently established 2011 general-studies paper, though republication, reuse, and incorrect website dates have not been independently ruled out. Do not reassign IDs, replace records, treat the third-party key as official, or fill Q7/Q38 on this basis. The bank, source documents, and prior checkpoints remain untouched. A verifiable original 2011 paper or a user decision on how to handle the disputed identity is needed before importing answers as 2011 PYQs.
