MPPSC PRELIMS 2016 — SOURCE QBANK PACKAGE

Contents
- MPPSC_Prelims_2016_QBank_Source.json: 100 normalized questions, options, printed answers, source explanations, verbatim source snippets, and preliminary classification.
- MPPSC_Prelims_2016_Review_Report.json: parsing checks, subject counts, and questions needing review.

Important
- This is an intake/source package, not the final MyMPPSC import schema.
- Subject/topic labels are preliminary and will be harmonized after the 2013–2018 papers are all processed.
- Printed answers and explanations are preserved from the supplied solved paper and have not been independently fact-checked.
- Review flags do not automatically mean the printed answer is wrong; they indicate a reason to inspect the source.
